About the Initiative!

X3

The IMPOSTOR INITIATIVE is a project originally started by the folks at MotorFrog, a small team behind the VS IMPOSTOR mod. It was planned for April Fools Day of 2024, being a series of games including (but not limited to) Tomongus Pinball, Dave's Dream Job, and Crewmate Crush! It soon got a Wave 2 announced in the IMPOSTOR DIRECT 2026 (which was just a joke.)

The INITIATIVE ended up being revived by me (MC28), and it is currently out now as a small demo!
